About

I study how language models store and use information. My current project, Seahorse, asks whether a frozen model can remember what you told it in an earlier conversation, and what breaks when it tries.

I've also worked on deep reinforcement learning, diffusion and state-space models, and I write long-form tutorials on them for Towards Data Science.

Seahorse: a memory for a frozen language model

A vector pushed into a model's activations carries leanings, but not facts. A stored two-position slice of its own attention memory carries facts, and breaks in specific ways.
